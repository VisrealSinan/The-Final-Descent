Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: first character concept sheet for Ghalrym, playable cat-form Druid in The Final Descent, a 2D dark-fantasy parody dungeon crawler.
Reference image role: attached Han artwork is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY for polished hand-painted fantasy rendering, rich materials, softly dramatic charcoal studio lighting and playful personality. Do not reproduce Han, bear anatomy, gnome face, beard, goggles or bear equipment.
User-defined character: Ghalrym is a male Druid who chooses CAT FORM above all other forms and even rests in it. He loves AI and anime cat girls and is fiercely loyal to friends. Depict him as a full quadrupedal FELINE, not a humanoid cat-boy or cat-girl.
Proposed visual direction for review: a sleek, athletic panther-sized fantasy cat with graceful muscular shoulders, long feline legs, strong soft paws, expressive almond-shaped turquoise eyes, a little tousled forehead fur, exactly two large tufted feline ears and one long flowing tail. His expression combines clever curiosity, affection and a hint of smug anime-protagonist confidence. Adult heroic cat, not a baby kitten. Distinctive silhouette: agile and elongated, with a modest fluffy cheek ruff and long elegant tail.
Proposed colors: silver-gray fur and pale muzzle/chest, muted midnight-violet markings along shoulders, back, legs and tail. Deep violet tail tip. Keep the palette grounded in dark fantasy, with restrained turquoise accents.
Proposed accessories: minimal dark violet leather collar and fitted shoulder harness, weathered bronze hardware, a small leaf-shaped chest clasp with delicate turquoise circuit-like inlay as a visual nod to his love of AI. One tiny enamel anime-catgirl mascot charm attached to the harness as a playful personal keepsake. The charm is a small decorative object, not a companion character; no abilities or technology powers implied. No goggles, no heavy armor, no weapons, no clothes covering the feline body. Keep gear sparse, consistent and readable.
Composition: landscape character design sheet with TWO complete full-body views of the SAME cat side by side. Left: front three-quarter standing on all four paws, clear face, chest and silhouette. Right: rear three-quarter with a slight look back, showing matching fur pattern, harness and entire long tail. Same proportions and accessories in both views. All ears, paws and tail tips fully within frame with generous margins and separation; no overlap between the two figures.
Art style: polished hand-painted 2D dark fantasy concept art, stylized World-of-Warcraft-inspired game silhouette and materials, expressive anime-influenced eyes while matching the reference's painted style. Original fantasy cat design suitable for later top-down sprite adaptation. Not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not pixel art.
Background: simple charcoal studio backdrop with faint grounding shadows.
No text, captions, UI, watermarks, logos, spell effects, robots, mechanical limbs, scenery, extra characters or copied recognizable anime characters. This is a first visual proposal, not a sprite sheet.
