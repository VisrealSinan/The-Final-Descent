THE FINAL DESCENT - VISREAL COMBAT PROTOTYPE

Windows x64. Extract the whole ZIP, then double-click TheFinalDescent.exe.
Keep TheFinalDescent.pck next to the executable. No Godot installation is needed.

Enter: begin / next fight / restart after death
WASD: move
Hold arrow keys: aim and repeatedly slash (two arrows allow diagonal aim)
Q: Shadowstep in the last arrow aim direction; empower your next slash
E: Venom Blades; temporarily coat swords to apply poison over time
Esc: pause / resume
R: reset the run, including gear
Close the window or use Alt+F4 to quit.

COMBO
Press E, slash an enemy to poison it, press Q, then slash again before the
empowerment expires. Nightshade adds damage to that empowered strike when
the target is already poisoned. The arrow at Visreal's feet shows his aim.
Red enemy arcs announce an incoming melee attack. Move out of reach to dodge.

EQUIPMENT
Five short fights each contain a guardian and two smaller melee enemies.
Each guardian automatically equips one random piece from the remaining slots:
  Paired swords: direct poison and shadow damage on melee hits
  Armor: reduced incoming damage
  Cape: shorter Shadowstep cooldown
  Mask: increased direct poison and poison-over-time damage
  Trinket: increased Shadowstep-empowered shadow damage
There are no duplicate rewards. After five fights, continue sparring with the
full kit or start over. Health and cooldowns reset between fights; gear does not.
Death ends the run. Restarting clears gear and reshuffles the rewards.

SCOPE
This is a playable single-room combat prototype with temporary balance values,
placeholder enemies, working equipment names, and no audio. The six-floor
dungeon, XP progression, Han/Ghalrym gameplay and class-themed finale are not
part of this build. The five test fights are not the five dungeon floors.

Built with Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, ChatGPT/Codex and OpenAI-generated Visreal
sprite assets. Source: https://github.com/VisrealSinan/The-Final-Descent

This prototype bundles the full Godot runtime for convenient testing; it is
larger than an optimized release export. Godot license: GODOT-LICENSE.txt.
